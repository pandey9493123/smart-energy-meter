Complete User Guide

Everything you need to know about setting up and using your Smart Energy Meter.

Getting Started

Welcome to Smart Energy Meter! Follow these simple steps to start monitoring your electricity in real-time.

  1. Create Your Account
    Click Sign Up on the homepage. Enter your name, email, password, device ID and tariff rate.
  2. Verify Your Device ID
    Your device ID (e.g. device001) links your hardware to your account.
  3. Login to Dashboard
    Use your credentials to access the live monitoring dashboard.
  4. Connect Your Hardware
    Follow the Hardware Setup guide below to install your ESP32 with sensors.
  5. Start Monitoring!
    Once connected, live data appears instantly on your dashboard.
Pro Tip: Enable browser notifications in your Profile to get instant fault alerts even when the dashboard is not open.

Dashboard Guide

The dashboard is your control center. Here's what each section does:

  • Live Stats Cards Shows real-time Voltage, Current, Power and Energy values updated every second.
  • Bill Estimation Auto-calculates daily and monthly bills based on your tariff rate.
  • Relay Control Turn your device ON/OFF remotely with a single click.
  • Fault Status Red alert banner appears when abnormal conditions are detected.
  • Monthly Goal Set a kWh target and track your progress with a visual progress bar.
  • Energy Tips Rotating tips to help you save more energy every day.
  • Live Charts Real-time graphs showing power trends and voltage/current variations.
  • Achievements Unlock badges as you use the system and hit milestones!

Hardware Setup

The Smart Energy Meter uses ESP32 as the main controller with voltage and current sensors.

Required Components:

  • ESP32 DevKit - Microcontroller with WiFi
  • ZMPT101B - Voltage sensor module (0-250V AC)
  • ZMCT103C - Current sensor (0-5A)
  • 5V Relay Module - For remote switching
  • Jumper Wires + Breadboard
  • USB Cable - For programming ESP32
Safety Warning: Working with AC mains voltage (230V) is dangerous. Always ensure the power is OFF before making any connections. If unsure, seek help from a qualified electrician.

Pin Connections (ESP32):

WIRING 
ZMPT101B (Voltage)  → GPIO 34
ZMCT103C (Current)  → GPIO 35
Relay Module IN     → GPIO 26
Common VCC          → 3.3V / 5V
Common GND          → GND

Reading the Data

Understanding what each measurement means will help you make sense of your consumption patterns.

  • Voltage (V) Standard India supply is 230V ± 10%. Values outside 200-250V may cause damage.
  • Current (A) Amount of electricity flowing. Higher current = more devices running.
  • Power (W) Real-time consumption. Formula: Power = Voltage × Current.
  • Energy (kWh) Total consumption over time. This is what your electricity bill is based on.
Example: A 60W bulb running for 10 hours consumes 60W × 10h = 600Wh = 0.6 kWh. At ₹6.5/kWh, that's ₹3.90 per day.

Relay Control

The relay lets you turn your connected device ON or OFF remotely from anywhere.

  1. Locate the Relay Toggle
    On the dashboard, find the "Relay Control" card.
  2. Toggle ON/OFF
    Click the switch to change state. Green = ON, Red = OFF.
  3. Confirmation
    A toast notification will confirm the action was successful.
Important: The relay should only be used with devices under its rated current capacity (usually 10A). For high-power appliances (AC, water heater), use a contactor with proper isolation.

Tariff & Billing

The system automatically calculates your electricity bill based on the tariff rate you set.

How Billing Works:

FORMULA 
Daily Bill   = Energy (kWh) × Tariff (₹/kWh)
Monthly Bill = Daily Bill × 30
Total Cost   = Energy Consumed × Your Tariff

Setting Your Tariff:

  1. Go to Profile Page
    Click on Profile in the sidebar.
  2. Find Tariff Settings
    Click Edit Profile and adjust the slider or type manually.
  3. Save Changes
    Click Update Tariff to save. Bills will recalculate instantly.
Tip: Check your last electricity bill for the exact tariff rate in your area. Rates vary by state and consumption slab.

Troubleshooting

Facing an issue? Here are quick fixes for common problems.

Device shows Offline
Fix: Check WiFi credentials in ESP32 code. Verify router is on. Restart device by unplugging for 10 seconds.
All values show 0
Fix: Hardware may not be connected to AC. Values only update when device is powered by mains supply.
Cannot Login
Fix: Verify email/password. Use "Forgot Password" if needed. Check if your domain is authorized in Firebase Console.
Relay not responding
Fix: Check ESP32 pin connections. Verify relay module has power. Refresh dashboard and try again.
Graphs not updating
Fix: Refresh the page. Check internet connection. Ensure Firebase database rules allow read access.
Photo upload fails
Fix: Image must be under 2MB. Enable Firebase Storage in your project. Check Storage rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Smart Energy Meter really free?
Yes! The web platform is 100% free. You only need to buy the hardware components (ESP32 + sensors) which cost around ₹800-1200 total.
Is my data secure?
Yes. We use Firebase Authentication (Google-owned) with industry-standard security. Your data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and only you can access your account.
How accurate are the readings?
The ZMPT101B and ZMCT103C sensors provide ±1% accuracy for voltage and ±2% for current when calibrated properly. Suitable for household monitoring but not for commercial billing.
Can I use it for multiple devices?
Currently one ESP32 per account (device001). You can install it on your main power line to monitor total home consumption, or on specific circuits.
Does it work without internet?
The hardware needs WiFi to send data to Firebase. Without internet, values won't update on the dashboard. However, the ESP32 buffers data locally and syncs when connection returns.
Can I export data for analysis?
Yes! Use the "Export CSV" button on the dashboard to download all readings. You can also generate a PDF report with charts included.
How do I change my tariff rate?
Go to Profile → Tariff Settings → Click Edit → Adjust slider or enter manually → Save. Bill calculations update immediately.
What browsers are supported?
All modern browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari, Opera. Works on desktop, tablet and mobile. For best experience, use latest version of Chrome.
