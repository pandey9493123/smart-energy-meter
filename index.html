What We Offer

Powerful Features

Everything you need to monitor, analyze, and optimize your energy consumption in one clean and smart dashboard.

Voltage Monitoring

Track your supply voltage in real-time. Identify fluctuations and over/under voltage conditions instantly.

Current Tracking

Monitor live current draw. Detect abnormal loads before they cause damage to your appliances.

Power Analysis

View real-time power consumption in Watts with live graphs that update every second.

Bill Estimation

Automatically calculate your daily and monthly electricity bill based on actual energy consumption.

Fault Detection

Instant fault alerts with visual warnings when abnormal conditions are detected in your circuit.

Remote Relay Control

Turn your connected device ON or OFF remotely from anywhere using the dashboard.

The Process

How It Works

Three simple steps from hardware to live data on your screen.

01

Connect Hardware

ESP32 + sensors are connected to your AC supply. Blynk IoT handles the hardware communication.

02

Data Syncs to Firebase

All readings (voltage, current, power, energy) sync to Firebase Realtime Database automatically.

03

Monitor on Dashboard

View live data, graphs, and bills on your personalized web dashboard from anywhere in the world.
